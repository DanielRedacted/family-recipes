Grandma's Apple Pie

A classic apple pie recipe passed down through generations. Serves 8.

Prep: 30 minutes | Bake: 55 minutes | Total: 1 hour 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 pre-made pie crusts (or homemade)
  • 6 large apples (Granny Smith or Honeycrisp), peeled and sliced
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons butter, cut into small pieces
  • 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Place one pie crust in a 9-inch pie dish.
  3. In a large bowl, toss sliced apples with sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt.
  4. Pour apple filling into crust-lined dish. Dot with butter pieces.
  5. Cover with second pie crust. Crimp edges to seal. Cut slits in top.
  6. Brush top crust with beaten egg.
  7. Bake 45–55 minutes until crust is golden and filling is bubbling.
  8. Cool for at least 2 hours before slicing.

Equipment

9-inch pie dish, large mixing bowl, pastry brush, apple peeler
