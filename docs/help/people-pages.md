Every recipe remembers who wrote it, who added it and who changed it. **People** gathers those names, so you can see what each person has given the family cookbook.

## Opening People

- **In the folder column**: the people button at the bottom, beside the calendar.
- **From a recipe**: tap the name after **By** under its title. Under **More**, the **Added by** name and the names in its **History** open their pages too.
- **From the whiteboard**: tap the name on a note.

## Who is listed

- **Family** (or your group's name, set in Settings → General → Group): everyone who signs in on a phone, tablet or computer.
- **Also in the book**: every other name a recipe credits, such as a grandparent whose recipes were typed up for them, or a cookbook a recipe came from. They have a page even though they never sign in.

Each name shows how many recipes credit them.

## A person's page

A page shows the person's picture and name, then:

- **Recipes**: the ones they wrote.
- **Added to the book**: ones they brought in but didn't write.
- **Edited**: ones they changed.
- **Notes**: the notes they left on recipes, newest first. Tap one to open the recipe at that note. Leaving a note doesn't count as writing or changing the recipe, so it never puts their name under **By** or in its history (see [Notes on recipes](#/settings/help/notes)).
- **Favorites**: the recipes they marked as favorites, for people who sign in (see [Bookmarks and favorites](#/settings/help/bookmarks-and-favorites)).

A page comes from the recipes themselves, so it stays as long as the recipes do, even if that person stops using the app. If someone's name changes, recipes with their earlier name still count as theirs.

## Your picture

On your own page, tap **Add a picture…** (or **Change picture…**). Choose a photo, or pick an emoji. A photo is cut to a square from its middle. **Remove** goes back to your initials.

Everyone in the family sees your picture. People who never sign in show their initials.

## Folding sections

Tap a section's heading, such as **Recipes** or **Favorites**, to fold it away, and tap it again to open it. This device remembers which sections you folded.
