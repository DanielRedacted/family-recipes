## What you see

When the recipe's author is known, a line under the title says who wrote it ("By …"). Below that is a row of details, such as yield, servings and times. Tap **More** to see the website the recipe came from, who added it, its tags and its history. Each ingredient's amount is shown in bold.

## Making more or less

Use **−** and **+** above the ingredients to scale the recipe. If the recipe says how much it makes, the label shows the new count, such as "9 servings" or "24 cookies (2 dozen)". Recipes counted in dozens go up and down by half a dozen. Otherwise the label shows a multiplier, such as ×1.5. Tap **Reset** to go back to the recipe as written.

Every list of ingredients in the recipe scales together. Amounts round to kitchen fractions such as ½ or ⅓. Things you can't use part of, such as eggs, cloves and cans, round to whole numbers. Only the amount at the start of each ingredient line changes. The steps stay as written.

## US or metric

Choose **Units** in **Settings → General**. **US** keeps the recipe's own units and swaps in a handier one when scaling makes an amount awkward (16 tbsp halved becomes ½ cup). **Metric** converts ingredient amounts to ml and g. The choice applies to this device only.

## Portions

Some recipes have a table with a column for each version, such as Single and Double. Chips above the ingredients let you pick one, and each chip shows how much it makes compared with the first (×2). Only that column's amounts are shown. When **+** reaches a column's size, the app shows the family's own amounts for that column.

## Links to other recipes

A link to another recipe opens it in the app. Links to websites are marked ↗ and open in your web browser. An ingredient with the same name as another recipe links to that recipe on its own (for example, "pie crust" opens Pie Crust). At the bottom of a recipe, **Mentioned in** lists the recipes that link to it.

## PDFs, pictures and other files

A PDF opens right in the app. Use **−** and **+** to zoom, or **Open in new tab** to see it on its own. Some recipes are kept only as their original PDF, which then shows under the title. If you can edit, **Convert to an editable recipe…** turns the PDF into a recipe you can scale and cook from (see [Importing recipes](#/settings/help/importing)). Pictures open full size.
## Printing

Tap the **⋯** button at the top of the recipe and choose **Print**. The page prints as you see it, scaled amounts included, but without the buttons.
