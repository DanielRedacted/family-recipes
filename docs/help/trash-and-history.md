Nothing you delete or change is lost straight away. Deleted recipes wait in the Trash, and the app keeps the version from before each save.

## Delete a recipe or folder

Open the recipe, tap **More** (the ⋯ button), and choose **Move to trash**. On a computer you can also right-click a recipe or folder in the list and choose **Move to trash**.

The app asks first. A folder goes to the Trash with everything inside it. You need editing rights to delete.

## Restore from the Trash

Go to **Settings → Trash**. It lists everything deleted, newest first, with the date it was deleted.

Tap **Restore** next to an item to put it back where it was. A restored recipe opens straight away. If another recipe with the same name is there now, the restored one gets a number added, like "Apple Pie (2)".

Things stay in the Trash until it's emptied, which can only be done on the Mac. Emptying the Trash removes deleted recipes and earlier versions for good.

## Recent changes

**Settings → Recent changes** shows the ten recipes changed most recently, with their folder and how long ago. Tap one to open it. It's the quickest way to find a recipe someone just added or edited.

## A recipe's history

Each time someone saves a recipe, the app asks **What changed?**. The answer (optional) is added to the recipe's history with their name and the date. Imports are recorded the same way, with where the recipe came from.

To see the history, open the recipe and tap **More** in the row with the servings at the top. The **History** list shows the newest change first.

## Earlier versions

Before a recipe is saved over, the app quietly keeps a copy of how it was (at most one copy every ten minutes). These earlier versions don't appear in the Trash list, and you can't bring one back from a phone. If you need an older version of a recipe, ask whoever looks after the Mac.

To change a recipe, see [Editing a recipe](#/settings/help/editing).
