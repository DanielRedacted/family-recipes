Most families have one cookbook, and the app shows just that. It can also keep several, each its own
collection: the family's recipes, say, and a separate one for canning or for trying things out.

## Choosing a cookbook

When there is more than one, the name at the top of the folder column is the cookbook you're in. Tap
it and choose another. On a phone, tap **Folders** first.

Each phone, tablet and computer keeps its own choice, so the Mac can show one cookbook while a phone
shows another. Everything on the screen then belongs to that cookbook: the folders, search,
**Random**, the home page, people's pages and the recipe book.

If a cookbook is taken away while you're in it, the app moves you to another.

## What every cookbook shares

- **People and the PIN**: you sign in once, whichever cookbook you're in.
- **The whiteboard**.
- **The meal history**: the Calendar shows every meal made, from any cookbook. A meal from another
  cookbook shows that cookbook's name and can't be opened from here. A dinner's protein isn't
  suggested again for a few days, whichever cookbook it came from.

## What each cookbook keeps to itself

- **Its recipes, folders and trash.**
- **Favorites**: a recipe you favorite in one cookbook isn't a favorite in another.
- **Feedback** sent from a cookbook is kept with it.
- **GitHub sync**: each can be kept in its own repository, or none.

## Adding one (on the Mac)

Open **Settings → Cookbooks** and choose **Open or create a cookbook…**. Pick a folder that already
holds recipes, or make a new folder in the window that opens. The new cookbook is ready at once.

Each cookbook there also has:

- **Show in Finder**.
- **Only on this Mac**: phones, tablets and other computers don't see it, or its meals on the
  Calendar.
- **GitHub**: its own sync with a private copy online.
- **Remove**: the app stops showing it. Its folder and recipes stay where they are on the Mac, and
  you can open it again later.

If a cookbook's folder has moved or is on a drive that isn't connected, it says so; **Choose its
folder…** points the app at where it is now.

A cookbook's name is its folder's name. To rename one, quit Recipes, rename the folder in Finder,
then open Recipes and use **Choose its folder…**.
