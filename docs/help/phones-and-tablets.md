The recipes live on the family Mac. Any phone, tablet or other computer on the same home Wi-Fi can open them in its web browser. There's nothing to install.

## Open the recipes

1. Connect to the home Wi-Fi.
2. In the browser, go to **recipes.local**. Or, on the Mac, open **Settings → Phones & tablets** and scan the QR code with your phone's camera.

If your device can't find recipes.local, ask whoever looks after the Mac: the same page on the Mac can show an address with numbers instead.

## Sign in

The first time, the app asks **Who's cooking?** Tap your name, or add yourself with your name and your full name (the full name goes on recipes you add or change). If the family uses a PIN, type it in **Family PIN** first.

The device remembers you. To sign out, go to **Settings → General → Sign out**.

Without a family PIN you can read recipes and use the whiteboard, but not edit, import or delete. If the PIN is changed, every device signs in again.

## Add it to the home screen

This one-time step makes Recipes a full-screen app on your home screen that keeps working for reading while the Mac is asleep. It's optional.

1. In the app, go to **Settings → General** and tap **Install as an app**.
2. Tap **Download certificate**. The certificate only lets your device recognise the Recipes app on the Mac; it works for nothing else.
3. Trust it. The page shows the steps for your kind of device. On an iPhone or iPad, use Safari: after downloading, open the Settings app, tap **Profile Downloaded**, then **Install**. Then go to **Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings** and switch on the Recipes certificate.
4. Back on the page, tap **Open https://recipes.local**.
5. Add it to the home screen. On an iPhone or iPad: the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**. On Android: the ⋮ menu, then **Install app**.

After this, the device always opens the secure address by itself.

## When the Mac is asleep or away

The home-screen app keeps the last copy it saw. While it can't reach the Mac, the home page says **Offline — showing the last saved copy**. You can still find and read recipes you've opened before, but you won't see new changes and you can't edit until the Mac is back.

## Getting around on a phone

On a phone the folder column is tucked away, and a bar along the bottom has three buttons:

- **Folders** slides out the folder column. Its bottom row has **Ingredients** and the **Settings** gear.
- **Home** goes to the home page, with the whiteboard and dinner ideas.
- **Search** finds recipes, folders and ingredients.

Settings sections appear as a row of buttons at the top of the Settings page.
