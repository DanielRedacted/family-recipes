The whiteboard is a place for quick kitchen notes everyone sees: "We're out of eggs", "Lasagne is in the freezer", "Dinner at 7 tonight".

## Where it is

The whiteboard sits on the home page, under the heading **Whiteboard**. It only appears while there's at least one note on it, with the newest note first.

Each note shows who wrote it, when (the time if it was today, otherwise the date), and how long it stays, such as **Today only** or "Until Fri, Nov 28".

## Write a note

1. Go to the home page (tap **Home** in the bottom bar on a phone).
2. Tap the speech-bubble button, **Write on the whiteboard**, at the top of the page.
3. Type your note. Keep it short: up to 500 characters.
4. Under **Keep it**, choose:
   - **Today only**: the note disappears at midnight.
   - **Until** a date: it stays through the end of the day you pick.
   - **Until someone removes it**: it stays until someone wipes it off.
5. Tap **Post**.

The note shows up straight away on every phone, tablet and computer that has the app open.

## Remove a note

Tap the **×** on a note, then **Remove**. Anyone who's signed in can remove any note, not only their own, just like a real whiteboard. Notes that have run out disappear by themselves.

## Who can use it

Everyone who's signed in with their name can write and remove notes, even when editing recipes isn't allowed on that device. Notes are kept on the Mac with the app, not with the recipes.
