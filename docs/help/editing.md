Editing needs the family PIN on phones and tablets (see [Getting started](#/settings/help/getting-started)).

## A new recipe

Open the folder the recipe belongs in, tap the **+** button at the top and choose **Blank recipe**. Type its name and tap **Create**. The recipe opens in the editor with headings for Ingredients, Directions and Notes. The same menu can also import a recipe (see [Importing recipes](#/settings/help/importing)), upload files or make a new folder.

## Changing a recipe

Open the recipe, tap the **⋯** button at the top and choose **Edit** (⌘E on the Mac). The bar above the text starts with two editor styles:

- **Formatted** looks like the finished recipe, much like Apple Notes. Use the bar to pick a text type (**Body**, **Title**, **Heading**, **Subheading**), and to add bold, italic, lists, checklists, quotes, tables and pictures. You can also drop or paste a picture into the recipe.
- **Markdown** shows the recipe's plain text, with all its marks.

Your device remembers the style you used last. While you type an ingredient, the app suggests ingredient names it knows.

Nothing is saved until you tap **Save** (or press ⌘S). Until then, the top of the page says **Unsaved changes**. **Done** goes back to reading. If you leave with unsaved changes, the app asks whether to **Save** or **Don't save**. On a wide screen, **⋯ → Show preview alongside** shows the finished recipe next to the editor.

## Details

You can add a missing detail (yield, servings, times, source) without opening the editor. Tap **More** under the title, tap the detail marked **+**, type it and press Enter. It's saved straight away. In the editor, the details sit in a block at the top of the recipe.

## Linking to another recipe

Type two square brackets, **[[**, and the app suggests recipe names. You can also use the link button in the bar. The linked recipe then lists this one under **Mentioned in**. To follow a link while you're editing, ⌘-click it.

To link a person, type **@** and start their name; to link an ingredient, type **#** and start its name. Pick one from the suggestions. In the recipe, “@Grandma” then reads as a link to their page and “#butter” as a link to the ingredient. Only names and ingredients the book already knows become links, so “#10 can” stays as it is. **[[** works for people and ingredients too when no recipe has that name.

## Who gets the credit

When you save, the app asks **What changed?** Type a short note, such as "Less sugar", or leave it blank, and tap **Save**. The recipe keeps three kinds of credit, shown under **More**:

- **Author**: who wrote the recipe, shown as "By …" under the title. Leave **Credit me as an author** ticked to add your name. It starts ticked unless someone else is already the author.
- **Added by**: who brought the recipe into the app. It's set once and never replaced.
- **History**: a dated list of who changed what. One person's changes on the same day share one entry.

On the Mac, the credit goes to the person chosen in **Editing as**, beside **Save**. Left on **Admin**, the change is credited to nobody.

## When someone else changed it too

If another device saves the recipe while you're editing, a bar says so. **Keep mine** saves your version, and **Use theirs** drops your changes. **Compare** shows both versions side by side. Arrows copy a change from theirs into yours. You can edit your side freely, then tap **Save my version**.

Earlier versions are kept too. See [Trash and history](#/settings/help/trash-and-history).
