The **Calendar** page shows the family's kitchen history a week or a month at a time: what was cooked, which recipes were added and what was changed. When it's turned on, it also shows the events from the calendar on the Mac.

## Opening it

Click the calendar button at the bottom of the folder column. On a phone, tap **Folders** first to find it. You can also tap **Calendar** beside **Today** on the home page.

- **Week** shows seven days side by side, Sunday to Saturday. On a phone the days are listed one under another.
- **Month** shows the whole month. Each day lists its first few entries; on a phone it shows how many there are. Tap a day to see everything on it underneath.
- **‹** and **›** go back and forward, and **Today** comes back to now.

## What it shows

- **Cooked**: a recipe someone marked **Made today**, with who and when. See [Cook mode](#/settings/help/cook-mode).
- **Probably cooked**, marked **Assumed**: a recipe that stayed open on someone's screen for 20 minutes without anyone tapping **Made today**. It's a guess, so it's greyed out. Tap **Yes, cooked** if it was, or **Not cooked** to remove it. Tapping **Made today** on the recipe that day also turns the guess into a real entry.
- **Added**: who added a recipe, from the day it was made in the app or imported.
- **Changed**: who changed a recipe, with what they wrote under "What changed?".
- **Events** from the Mac's calendar, on a blue background, with their time and place.

Recipes added or changed before the app kept a history, or changed outside the app, don't appear.

A guess never stops the home page from suggesting a dinner. Only a real **Made today** does that.

With more than one [cookbook](#/settings/help/cookbooks), the Calendar shows the meals made from all of them. A meal from another cookbook shows that cookbook's name, and opens only from there. **Added** and **Changed** show the cookbook you're in.

## Today's events on the home page

When the Mac's calendar is turned on and there's something on today, the home page shows a **Today** list under the whiteboard. On a day with no events, the list isn't there.

## The Mac's calendar

The events come from the Calendar app on the Mac that runs Recipes, and only someone at that Mac can turn them on: **Settings → Calendar → Show events from this Mac's calendar**. The first time, the Mac asks whether Recipes may see the calendar. Then tick the calendars to show, such as a family calendar. Nothing shows until you tick one, and a calendar you add later stays hidden until you tick it. The Mac only lets an app see all of its calendars or none, but Recipes reads only the ones ticked here.

Recipes only reads the calendar. To add, move or delete an event, use the Calendar app, or anything that syncs with it such as a phone's calendar. Recipes shows the change the next time you open the Calendar or the home page, or come back to them from another app; a page left open checks every 15 minutes.

Everyone signed in sees the events, on the Mac and on phones and tablets.

If Settings says Recipes isn't allowed to see the calendar, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars** on the Mac, turn Recipes on, and open the Calendar settings again.
