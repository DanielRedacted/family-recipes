To open Settings, click the gear button at the bottom of the folder column, or press ⌘, on the Mac. On a phone, tap **Folders** first to find the gear. The sections are listed down the left. On phones and narrow windows they're a row of buttons at the top of the page. To get back to the recipes, use **‹ Recipes** at the top of the list or **Home** on a phone.

Not everyone sees every section. Some only appear in the Recipes app on the Mac.

## For everyone

- **General**: **Theme** (Light or Dark) and **Units** (US or Metric), saved on this device. On a phone or tablet, **This device** shows who you're signed in as, says whether you can only read, and has **Sign out**. It may also offer **Install as an app** (see [Phones and tablets](#/settings/help/phones-and-tablets)).
- **Recent changes**: the ten recipes changed most recently, and when.
- **Trash**: deleted recipes and files, each with **Restore**. You only see this if you can edit. The trash can only be emptied on the Mac. See [Trash and history](#/settings/help/trash-and-history).
- **Help**: these pages. Use **Search help** to find a page that mentions a word.

## Only on the Mac

- **Recipes folder**: where the recipes are kept on the Mac, with **Show in Finder** and **Use a different folder…**. Under **GitHub**, the recipes can be kept in sync with a private copy online. Once connected, it has **Sync now** and **Stop syncing**.
- **Desktop app**: **Open Recipes at login**, and **Check for updates**. Closing the window keeps Recipes running in the menu bar, so phones and tablets can still reach it.
- **Phones & tablets**: **Share recipes with devices on this Wi-Fi**, a QR code a phone can scan to open Recipes, and **Keep this Mac awake while sharing**. Here you also set, change or remove the **Family PIN** that lets other devices edit.
- **People**: everyone who has signed in, with their devices and the changes they made. **Add a person…**, **Rename…**, **Full name…** and **Remove** look after the list. Here you also choose who the Mac is **Editing as**. **Changes made on this Mac** lists the Mac's own changes.
- **Calendar**: **Show events from this Mac's calendar**, off unless it's turned on here, and which calendars to show (none until you tick one). Recipes only reads the calendar; events are changed in the Calendar app. See [The calendar](#/settings/help/calendar).
- **Nutrition**: an optional free **USDA API key**, kept in the Keychain. With it, the app looks up ingredients that aren't in its nutrition list yet (only the ingredient names are sent to USDA), and **Change…** on the Ingredients page can search USDA. See [Ingredients](#/settings/help/ingredients).
- **AI assistant**: off unless it's turned on here. When it's on, AI can suggest tidier formatting for a recipe or answer a question about it. These options appear in a recipe's **⋯** menu, and a suggestion is only saved if you accept it.
