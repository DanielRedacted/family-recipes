## What you see

When the recipe's author is known, a line under the title says who wrote it ("By …"). Below that is a row of details, such as yield, servings and times. Tap **More** to see the website the recipe came from, who added it, its tags and its history. Each ingredient's amount is shown in bold.

## Making more or less

Use **−** and **+** above the ingredients to scale the recipe. If the recipe says how much it makes, the label shows the new count, such as "9 servings" or "24 cookies (2 dozen)". Recipes counted in dozens go up and down by half a dozen. Otherwise the label shows a multiplier, such as ×1.5. Tap **Reset** to go back to the recipe as written.

Every list of ingredients in the recipe scales together. Amounts round to kitchen fractions such as ½ or ⅓. Things you can't use part of, such as eggs, cloves and cans, round to whole numbers. Only the amount at the start of each ingredient line changes. The steps stay as written.

## US or metric

Choose **Units** in **Settings → General**. **US** keeps the recipe's own units and swaps in a handier one when scaling makes an amount awkward (16 tbsp halved becomes ½ cup). **Metric** shows flour, sugar, butter and other solids in grams, liquids such as milk, water and oil in ml, and oven and other temperatures in °C. The choice applies to this device only.

## Versions

Some recipes hold two versions of a dish in one, such as two mug cakes. A button for each version sits at the top of the recipe; tap one to see just that version's ingredients and steps. Cook mode and Nutrition show the same version.

## Portions

Some recipes have a table with a column for each version, such as Single and Double. Chips above the ingredients let you pick one, and each chip shows how much it makes compared with the first (×2). Only that column's amounts are shown. When **+** reaches a column's size, the app shows the family's own amounts for that column.

## Nutrition

Tap **Nutrition** beside **More** at the top of the recipe to see its Nutrition Facts label, worked out from its ingredients, with the ingredients listed under it by weight as on a package. It scales with **−** and **+**. In a recipe that holds two versions, the label is for the version you're looking at. See [Ingredients](#/settings/help/ingredients) for how it's worked out and what it leaves out.

## Notes

Select any words in a recipe to leave a note on them, and read everyone else's under the recipe. See [Notes on recipes](#/settings/help/notes).

## Links to other recipes

A link to another recipe opens it in the app. Links to websites are marked ↗ and open in your web browser. An ingredient with the same name as another recipe links to that recipe on its own (for example, "pie crust" opens Pie Crust). At the bottom of a recipe, **Mentioned in** lists the recipes that link to it.

## PDFs, pictures and other files

A PDF opens right in the app. Use **−** and **+** to zoom, or **Open in new tab** to see it on its own. Some recipes are kept only as their original PDF, which then shows under the title. If you can edit, **Convert to an editable recipe…** turns the PDF into a recipe you can scale and cook from (see [Importing recipes](#/settings/help/importing)). Pictures open full size. An imported recipe keeps the file it came from: open it from the recipe's **⋯** menu, **Original file**. Word documents can't be shown in the app, so their page has a **Download** button.
## Printing

Tap the **⋯** button at the top of the recipe and choose **Print**. The page prints as you see it, scaled amounts included, but without the buttons, and in black on white even with the dark theme on. To print or save every recipe at once, see [The recipe book](#/settings/help/recipe-book).
