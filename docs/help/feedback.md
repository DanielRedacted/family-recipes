Found something that isn't working, or have an idea to make the app better? You can send it from any page, and it goes straight to whoever looks after the app.

## Send feedback

There's no button for it, so it never gets in the way. Instead:

- **On a phone or tablet:** tap **Search** and type **feedback** (or **bug** or **idea**). Tap **Send feedback…** at the top of the list.
- **On a computer:** press ⇧⌘F (Ctrl-Shift-F on Windows), or search for **feedback** the same way.
- **In Settings → Help:** tap **Send feedback** under the list of pages.

Then:

1. Choose **Something's wrong**, **An idea** or **Other**.
2. Write what happened, or what you'd like. For a problem, say what you expected to happen too.
3. Tap **Send feedback**. ⌘↩ sends it from the keyboard.

## What gets sent with it

**Include what I was looking at** is ticked to start with. It adds the recipe or page you were on, any text you had selected, and what kind of device you're using, so it's easier to find the problem. You can see exactly what will be added under the tick box. Untick it to send only your words.

Tip: select the words a problem is about (a wrong amount, say) before you open the form, and they'll be included.

Your name goes with your feedback.
