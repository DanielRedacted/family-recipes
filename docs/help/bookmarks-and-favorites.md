Recipes has two ways to mark a recipe you like. They work differently, so pick the one that fits.

## Bookmarks: for everyone

A bookmark is shared. When you bookmark a recipe, everyone sees it, on every device.

- **To bookmark a recipe**, open its **⋯** menu and choose **Bookmark for everyone**. Choose **Remove bookmark** to undo it.
- Bookmarked recipes are listed under **Bookmarks** on the home page and at the top of the folder column. They show a bookmark icon on their cards, and a **Bookmarked** label in their details.
- When you search, bookmarked recipes rank a little higher than other close matches.
- On the home page, the **Bookmarked** switch makes **Random** pick only from bookmarked recipes.

Bookmarking changes the recipe itself, so you need to be able to edit (see [Getting started](#/settings/help/getting-started)).

## Favorites: just yours

A favorite is personal. Nobody else sees your favorites, and you don't need the family PIN to keep them.

- **To add a favorite**, open the recipe's **⋯** menu and choose **Add to favorites**. Choose **Remove from favorites** to undo it.
- Your favorites are listed under **Favorites** at the top of the home page, and show a star on their cards.

Favorites are kept on this device, for the person signed in. If you use a phone and a tablet, each keeps its own list. If someone else signs in on the same device, they see their own favorites, not yours. If a recipe is renamed or moved on another device, it may drop out of your favorites, and you can add it again.

## Where to find the ⋯ menu

- **On a recipe page**: the **⋯** button at the top right.
- **On a card**: the **⋯** button in its corner. On the Mac, it shows when you point at the card. On a phone or tablet, it's always there.
- **In the folder column**: the **⋯** at the end of a row.
- **On the Mac**: right-click a card, a list row or a recipe in the folder column.
