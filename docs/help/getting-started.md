Recipes is the family cookbook. The recipes live on the family Mac, and the Recipes app there shows them. Phones, tablets and other computers on the home Wi-Fi open the same recipes in their web browser, at the address shown on the Mac. Nothing needs to be installed. To set up a phone or tablet, see [Phones and tablets](#/settings/help/phones-and-tablets).

## Signing in on a phone or tablet

The first time you open Recipes on a device, it asks **Who's cooking?**

1. If the family has a PIN, type it in **Family PIN**.
2. Tap your name. If you're new, type a short name (such as "Ann") and your full name, then tap **Add me**. If nobody has signed in yet, the button says **Start**.

Your full name is used to credit you in the recipes you add or change. The device remembers you, so you only do this once. To sign out, go to **Settings → General** and tap **Sign out** under **This device**.

## Reading or editing

What you can do depends on whether a family PIN has been set on the Mac:

- **With a PIN**, everyone who signs in with it can edit, add, import, bookmark and delete recipes.
- **Without a PIN**, phones and tablets are read-only. You can still read recipes, cook from them, use cook mode, log **Made today**, keep your own favorites and write on the whiteboard. **Settings → General** says "Read-only" when this is the case.

If you try to edit without the PIN, the app tells you that editing needs a family PIN. When a PIN is set or changed, every device signs in again.

## On the Mac: "Editing as"

The Mac app never asks anyone to sign in. Instead, it credits its changes to the person chosen in **Editing as**. You'll find it beside **Save** while you edit a recipe or import one, and in **Settings → People**. It starts on **Admin**, which credits nobody; pick your name before you save and the app remembers it. Until someone has been added in **Settings → People**, the choice doesn't appear and changes made on the Mac aren't credited to anyone.
