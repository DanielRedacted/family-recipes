## Open the Kitchen charts

Tap the ruler button at the bottom of the folder column. On a phone, tap **Folders** in the bottom bar first.

The charts come with the app. They're the same in every cookbook, and nobody has to keep them up to date.

## Convert an amount

Type an amount and pick its unit. The same amount appears in every other unit of that kind:

- **Volume**: teaspoons, tablespoons, fluid ounces, cups, pints, quarts, gallons, millilitres and litres.
- **Weight**: ounces, pounds, grams and kilograms.
- **Temperature**: °F and °C.

Fractions work: type 1 ½, ⅓ or 1/3. An amount that doesn't come out even says "about" ("about 3½ oz"). Units too small to measure that much in are left out.

Cups and spoons can't be turned into grams here, because that depends on the ingredient: a cup of flour weighs much less than a cup of sugar. To see recipes in grams, choose **Metric** under **Units** in **Settings → General** (see [Reading a recipe](#/settings/help/reading-a-recipe)).

## The charts

- **Measures**: how many teaspoons, tablespoons, cups, fluid ounces and millilitres make up each common measure, from a teaspoon to a gallon. A blank box means nobody would measure it that way.
- **Weights**: ounces, pounds and grams.
- **Oven temperatures**: 250°F to 500°F with the °C setting for each. These match what a recipe shows in Metric.
- **Safe cooking temperatures**: the lowest temperature the thickest part of the food should reach, measured with a food thermometer, from the USDA's chart. Some meats should rest 3 minutes afterwards.

Your own charts, such as BBQ cooking times, can go in a folder of their own with the recipes, like any other page.
