Classic Chicken Noodle Soup
Serves: 6
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 45 minutes

A comforting homemade chicken noodle soup perfect for cold days.

Ingredients:
- 1 whole chicken (about 3 lbs), cut into pieces
- 3 medium carrots, sliced
- 3 stalks celery, chopped
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 8 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups egg noodles
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon dried parsley
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 bay leaves

Instructions:
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat.
2. Season chicken pieces with salt and pepper. Brown on all sides, about 5 minutes. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same pot, sauté onion, carrots, and celery until softened, about 5 minutes.
4. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute more.
5. Return chicken to the pot. Add broth, thyme, parsley, and bay leaves.
6. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes until chicken is cooked through.
7. Remove chicken. Shred meat and discard bones and bay leaves.
8. Return shredded chicken to pot. Add egg noodles and cook 8-10 minutes until tender.
9. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve hot.

Equipment needed: large pot, cutting board, chef's knife
