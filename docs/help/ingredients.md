## Open the Ingredients page

Tap **Ingredients** at the bottom of the folder column. On a phone, tap **Folders** in the bottom bar first.

Search finds ingredients too: type a name and pick the line marked "ingredient", and the Ingredients page opens on it.

## What you see

The page lists every ingredient used in your recipes, each with how many recipes use it and a little food picture where one fits. The list is built from the recipes themselves, so it keeps up as recipes are added or changed. Nobody has to maintain it.

Different spellings of the same thing are grouped together. "Eggs", "egg" and "2 large eggs, beaten" all count as eggs.

- Type in **Find an ingredient…** to narrow the list.
- Choose **Most used** or **A–Z** to change the order.
- Tap an ingredient to open it. You see the other ways it's written ("Also written as") and a list of every recipe that uses it, with its folder. Tap a recipe to open it.

This is a handy way to answer "what can I make with buttermilk?"

## Suggestions while you type

When you edit a recipe's ingredient list, the app suggests units after you type an amount, and then ingredient names it already knows from your other recipes. Pick one to save typing and keep spellings the same.

## Nutrition

Some recipes come with nutrition facts, usually ones imported from a recipe website that publishes them. When a recipe has them, a **Nutrition** table appears below the recipe when you read it, with the serving size beside the heading when it's known.

The numbers are copied from where the recipe came from; the app doesn't work them out. Recipes typed in by hand or imported from a document usually have none. When you import a recipe, you can see its nutrition on the **Preview** tab before saving (see [Importing recipes](#/settings/help/importing)).
